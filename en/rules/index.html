Community rules

They apply in the chat, the channel and at meetups.

  1. The community’s languages are Ukrainian and English.
  2. Respect each other. Insults, harassment and discrimination are not allowed.
  3. We discuss C++ and everything related to it: tools, builds, neighbouring languages, careers. Off-topic is fine in moderation.
  4. Value other people’s time. Ask without meta messages and don’t split your question up. Share code as text or a link, with all the context.
  5. Job posts, courses and ads only with the admins’ approval.
  6. Spam or an anti-Ukrainian stance gets you banned.

Admins may delete messages and restrict or ban those who break the rules. Send complaints to the chat admins or to hello@cpp.org.ua.
